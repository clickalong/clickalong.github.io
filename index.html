An AI support agent that finishes the task

Most AI customer support tools stop at the answer. Clickalong's AI support agent reads your docs, replies in your user's language, then guides them through your app one click at a time.

When it can't help, the chat goes to your team's shared inbox, so it works as an AI help desk too. The same widget runs product tours and user onboarding, so new users get walked through setup and everyone else gets shown where a feature lives the moment they ask.
